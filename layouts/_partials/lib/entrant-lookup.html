{{- /* Input: dict {season_id, name}: a name from data/groups/season-.yaml or a hand-entered side in data/brackets/season-.yaml. Output: the team of that name in the season's data/teams.yaml roster (a 2v2 season, Season 5) as lib/side-lookup.html gives it, so its slug matches the team's side in the match files; otherwise a player-lookup dict.
