## Bingo Brawlers

Two players (red and blue) each race to complete goals on a shared 5x5 bingo board. Completing a goal claims its square for your color. A game ends when a player completes a full line (row, column, or diagonal) or, if neither does, whichever color holds the majority of claimed squares when time runs out.
