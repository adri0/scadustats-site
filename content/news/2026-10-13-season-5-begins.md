### Season 5 transcription in progress

[Season 5](/seasons/5/) has started arriving! The first seven matches are up, nearly half of the 15-match round robin. The remaining twelve matches, up to the Grand Final, are next.

It took a bit longer because Season 5 was played in 2v2. Each team has one player in the base game and one in the DLC, and the board mixes base game, DLC and co-op squares, which need both teammates to do their part. So the site learned a few new tricks:

- Match pages show each team with its two players and which game each one played.
- [Player](/players/) pages count each mark for the teammate whose game the square was in, and there's a new Co-op list.
- The [Squares](/squares/) page has the co-op squares too.

### Revamped Matches and Squares pages

The [Matches](/matches/) page is now one list of every match, with a bar on top to filter by season or player and sort by date, newest first by default. Try [only Season 5](/matches/?season=5). The [Squares](/squares/) page got the same treatment: filter by type or text, and sort by most claimed, claim rate and more. Filtered lists keep their choices in the link, so you can share them.
