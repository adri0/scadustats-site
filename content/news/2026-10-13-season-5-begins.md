### Season 5 transcription in progress

Season 5 has started arriving! The first seven matches are up, that's the first half of the round robin, and the remaining twelve matches, up to the Grand Final, are next.

It took a bit longer because Season 5 was done with 2v2 matches. Each team has one player in the base game and one in the DLC, and the board mixes base game, DLC and co-op squares, which need both teammates to do their part. So the site learned a few new tricks:
- Match pages show each team with its two players and which game each one played.
- Player pages count each mark for the teammate whose game the square was in, and there's a new co-op list.
- The Squares page has the co-op squares too.

### Revamped Matches and Square pages

The Matches page is now one list of every match, with a bar on top to filter by season or player and sort by date. By default Matches are sorted from latest to earliest. The Squares page got the same treatment: filter by type or text, and sort by most claimed, claim rate and more. Filtered lists keep their choices in the link, so you can share them.
