[Season 5](/seasons/5/) is complete! All 19 matches are on the site: the 15 round robin games between the six teams, then the playoffs, where every match was a best of three: two semi-finals, the match for 3rd place and the Grand Final. That's 24 games of 2v2 bingo you can replay square by square.

The season page now shows how it all played out. The round robin is a grid of every team against every other, with each result linked to its match, and the playoffs are drawn as a bracket from the semi-finals to the Grand Final. The final placings are in the standings, and with no-spoilers mode on, all of it stays hidden until you're ready.

The Season 5 players have their avatars now too, and [Season 6](/seasons/6/) got its two missing matches, so all 40 are on the site.
