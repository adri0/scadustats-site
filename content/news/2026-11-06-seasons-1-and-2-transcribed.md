### Seasons 1 and 2 transcribed and available

With these two, every Bingo Brawlers season so far is on the site, from the very first match in 2023 to Season 6.

- [Season 1](/seasons/1/) is where it all started: eight players, 1v1, base game only, with a round robin and best-of-3 playoffs for the top four. Back then, each player claimed squares in their own bingosync colour, and the match pages now draw the boards in those colours.
- [Season 2](/seasons/2/) kept the 1v1 base-game format, with a round robin and playoffs for the top three.

Both season pages show the standings under that season's own rules (draws were possible) and the playoff bracket. One round-robin match in each season has no VOD: its result counts in the standings, but there's no match page for it.

Playoff games that were streamed as separate videos now link to their own VOD from the [match page](/matches/season-1/2023-05-06-ginomachino-vs-star0chris/).

### New calendar

The [calendar](/calendar/) shows every match played, the start and end of each season, and upcoming matches, as a month grid and a list. It's also on the home page.

### Seasons on the squares pages

- The [Squares](/squares/) page has a Season filter: pick a season and the figures switch to that season's.
- Each square page has a Seasons table with its numbers season by season.
